Overwritten to stay current (loopers and agents). Last updated: 2026-11-06 by the looper (#13).

- **Version / line:** unreleased (no tag yet, #2); tacplus floor 2.6, pinned to 2.6.1 in `tacplus.lock` (#13)
- **Works:** the tenant-owned C2S service `Doppelganger` (sessions, answers, user and tenant context), the model through llmproxy, context, sessions and panel runs as stratum records, written through stratum's record write surface (`InsertIfAbsent`, `ReplaceIfUnchanged` on the revision read and redone on `ABORTED`, `Apply` for a context; `DURABILITY_LOCAL`) and read with `Get` and the rows, each call routed to the request's tenant (`target_tenant`); nothing is published on the event bus, and a write refused while the redo is unreachable answers `UNAVAILABLE` (#7). Panel mode's doppelganger half (#10): `GeneratePanelAnswer` (two phases over `panel_model_aliases`, principles from `principles_paths` only, plain-code tally with dissent), a moderator (`panel_moderator_alias`: frames, merges and summarizes, each checked by code, falling back to code), byte-identical inputs for every seat, the resolved model id (llmproxy's `provider_model`) on every answer, `PanelRun` records, `UpdatePanelDecision` and `GetPanelTrackRecord`, tested against a fake provider. Every rpc routes by its `tenant` field (`route_field`, the caller's own tenant; #6), tested through the generated wrapper and the chassis backstop: a call reaches the named tenant's instance, a missing, malformed or other tenant's tid is refused. `just gates` pass through the looper's `tacplus` wrapper (2.6.1, #13); on 2.5 the build no longer resolves (stratum's floor is 2.6). Every handler forwards its request's `trace_id` to each call it causes (llmproxy, every panel seat and the moderator, retries included; the record store's reads and writes), tested per handler (#12).
